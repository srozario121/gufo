### Why

151.1 T5 could not complete leg P (the deployment's mikealanni-gufo baseline note). In the pin's warm-up 35 of 36 units got no prefix hit. The harness cuts the prefix and the
full context separately to 24,000 characters (`turns()` in the deployment's A/B harness (gufo_port_ab.py)); 34 of 36 contexts reach that cut, so the two start at different offsets and share
nothing past the header. The run was stopped instead of redesigned on the fly. Until this is fixed there is no prefill measurement of the re-cut image, 151.3's WMMA gate has
no working prefill leg, and the prefill gap to gufo's published 1,557 to 1,727 tok/s (ours 1,045 to 1,114, the deployment's gufo-throughput-headroom evaluation) cannot be attributed to
engine version. Item 152 (it stays in the ai-sdlc deployment repository) investigates the rest of that gap.
Item 152's report cites this item's +5.87% for the engine-version slice of prefill:
gufo-readme-bench.md (private evidence, ai-sdlc item 152) section 4.1.

### Design decisions (resolved)

- **The fix** `[default]`: cut the full context once to its last 24,000 characters (as now) and
  derive the prefix from it by removing the final message's rendering and one separator.
  Evidence: offline, `turns()` gives 2 of 36 prefixes that are true prefixes and 34 of 36
  full contexts at the 24,000 cut (reproduced in this refinement, matching `baseline.md`
  section 3); the full text is unchanged by the fix, so legs S and C and every number
  already reported stay comparable.
- **The unit stays per-turn prefill tokens per second of the uncached tail** `[default]`,
  to confirm: that is preregistration section 5. Evidence that it is fragile: with no cut,
  the 36 final messages (the tails) are 13 to 8,358 characters (median about 400, eight of
  them exactly 2,014, the tool-result cap), so most units are tens to a few hundred tokens
  and a per-turn rate on them is dominated by fixed overhead. So a pooled rate (sum of
  tail tokens over sum of tail seconds) and the TTFT are reported beside it, not gated.
  Alternative: make the pooled rate primary (a design change; would be `[override]`).
- **That the cache works on this model is a gate, not an assumption** `[default]`: one of
  the 36 warm-up units had a prefix hit, so a hit is possible, but cached tokens have not
  been compared with the prefix length. T4 does that on 3 turns before the screen.
- **Screen, not the 18-visit run** `[user]`: the user deferred the full run until the ports
  are gathered (2026-10-23). Pattern `ABBAABBA` (8 visits, 4 per arm, null 2 against 2)
  `[default]`, to confirm: leg P is cheap (36 short requests plus 36 prefix fills per visit,
  about 4 to 5 minutes with the 26 s start, estimated, not measured), so 8 visits plus the
  warm-up fit about 40 minutes, and the null gets two visits per half where 151.2's `ABBA`
  screen got one. Alternative: `ABBA` (about 22 minutes, null 1 against 1). Either is
  labelled "screen, not judged against the +3% bar"; no verdict is drawn.
- **Voiding rule** `[default]`, to confirm: a unit with no prefix hit is a failed unit
  (section 5). A visit with more than 4 of 36 failed units voids that leg's analysis (the
  rows are reported). Evidence: section 5 gives no number, and no more than 0 structural
  failures are expected after the fix; the threshold is written into A4 before the run.
- **A turn with an empty prefix** `[default]`: counted as a failed unit and kept (the 36
  are item 149's selection and not replaced). T2 counts them; none is expected (no tail
  reaches 24,000 characters; the longest is 8,358).
- **Where the fix lives** `[default]`: in `turns()` itself (a small pure helper that the
  selftest calls), not a new leg, so there is one definition of the contexts. No new Make
  target; `gufo-port-ab-selftest` already has its `check_changed.map` row
  (the deployment's A/B harness (gufo_port_ab.py)).
- **The 131k reading** stays out of scope `[default]`: preregistration section 8 keeps it
  reported-not-gated and it belongs to 151.3's microbench.
- **Order with item 152** `[default]`: no dependency. Both need `serve-outage`; they take
  separate leases. 152 may cite this result for engine-version attribution of the prefill
  gap (pin against re-cut); this item makes no claim about gufo's published figures. 152
  is not edited.
- **Split** `[default]`: not required (complexity 6, L). Two PRs: the first (T1 to T3, code
  and addendum, merged before the run) and the second (T4 to T6, results).

### Risks (pre-mortem)

Assume it shipped `partial`. Why?

- **The cache does not hit as assumed on the hybrid model** (cached tokens zero or far
  below the prefix length): T4 stops the lease early with the evidence; the item closes
  honestly as "fixed, not measurable, because ...", and 151.3's gate takes its own timing.
  Accepted named risk with that fallback.
- **Short tails make the per-turn rate noise**: the pooled rate and TTFT are reported; the
  result is only ever "screen, not judged", so a noisy leg does not become a verdict.
- **The A/A null fails or is weak** (2 against 2): reported "null failed" and not judged;
  the full run is deferred anyway. Accepted.
- **No lease window, or the restore is not verified**: T4 and T5 share one lease; the
  restore (status, `gufo.sh check`, config_hash) is in T5's acceptance and the item stays
  `in-progress` if it is not shown.
- **The fix changes the full contexts and breaks comparability with S and C**: T1 requires
  byte-identical full contexts and T2 measures it on the real sessions.
- **Concurrent lease use by item 152**: leases are exclusive; wait. No plan change.
- **Host differences** (macOS gates, GNU-only tools): the selftest is pure Python; the live
  half runs on the Linux box. Gate counts are quoted; live legs are not claimed until run.
